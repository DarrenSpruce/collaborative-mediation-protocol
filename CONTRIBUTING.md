# Contributing

Contribute anonymised scenarios, counterexamples, clearer instructions or evaluation results through issues and pull requests. Remove names, confidential details and identifying combinations of facts. Obtain permission before sharing another person's private material.

Distinguish reported events from synthetic scenarios. Describe the input, expected behaviours, unacceptable behaviours and why the case matters. Include cases where the mediator's first interpretation is wrong.

For evaluations, record the protocol version or commit, model identifier, exact prompts and outputs, settings when available, and reviewer rationale. Compare with a baseline without the protocol. Do not present unrun tests as results.

Preserve the distinction between observation and inferred motive. Do not infer consent from silence or judge competence from personality labels or cultural identity.

Contributions are offered under this repository's MIT licence.
